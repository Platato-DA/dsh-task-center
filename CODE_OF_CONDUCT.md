# Community guidelines

We welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Chinese and
English are both welcome.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code; avoid personal attacks.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or disclose another person's private
  information.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Do not post spam or
  credentials.
- Respect a maintainer's request to stop a conversation or move it elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or limit
participation. If a public report would expose private information, contact the
maintainer through the contact options they publish on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Platato-DA). Security vulnerabilities should
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
